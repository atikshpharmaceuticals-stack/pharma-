Commercial Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

What licensing documents are required for trade purchasing?

Customers and distribution partners require a valid Wholesale Drug License (Form 20B/21B) and an active GST Registration Certificate for commercial billing.

How can institutions request bulk pharmaceutical supply?

Please submit your required formulations, batch volumes, and delivery location via our inquiry form above. Our institutional supply desk will review and provide a formal quotation within 24 hours.

What is the typical delivery turnaround for in-stock formulations?

Standard catalog items in active inventory are dispatched via express transport partners within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.
